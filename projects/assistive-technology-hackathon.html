Project · Assistive Technology

Intercollegiate Assistive Technology Hackathon

April 2025 — Co-designing a custom switch holder so Hamza can play his favorite PlayStation games with his feet.

Co-design Laser cutting CAD $100 budget

The challenge

For the Intercollegiate Assistive Technology Hackathon, my team and I co-designed a custom switch holder for Hamza, a video game enthusiast with specific mobility needs. We worked closely with him to understand his anatomy, preferences, and goals, then developed a stable, adjustable, and budget-friendly solution that supports his switches at a comfortable angle in bed.

Our design: a longboard with Velcro on one side and a personalized graphic with Hamza's name on the other. Four adjustable panels attach to the Velcro, each with additional Velcro to secure the switches — letting Hamza play his favourite PlayStation games with his feet.

My contribution

I contributed to brainstorming, prototyping, and fabricating the final design — a Velcro-based board with customizable panels — while staying within a $100 budget. It was a meaningful experience applying engineering to improve real lives.
