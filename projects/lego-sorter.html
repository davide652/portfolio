Lego Sorter — Assistive Device

Natalie's cerebral palsy limits her mobility and fine motor skills, which makes it difficult for her to sort Legos. This solution allows her to sort Legos by herself with minimal mistakes and increased speed — maximizing building time with her brother Jason.

After the end of the project, my group and I participated in an in-person showcase to show our clients the final product. Testing went great and Natalie loved our creation. We were really proud of the final product.
